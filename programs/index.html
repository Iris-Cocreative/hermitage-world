A path that turns, not a ladder

There are many ways into the Hermitage, and no one has to take them in order. Seven stages, as Samuel Robinson describes the experience of arriving and staying.

  1. Discover

    Short teachings, the Justin archive, articles and the free healing meditations.

  2. Experience

    Meditate with us online, introductory evenings, open celebrations and livestreams.

  3. Explore

    One-off workshops and talks with Hermitage teachers and guests.

  4. Practice

    Four- to eight-week programs: Full Living, Train Your Mind, book groups.

  5. Belong

    Weekly groups, The Way Between, shared meals and the online community.

  6. Deepen

    Day and weekend retreats, advanced programs and spiritual formation.

  7. Serve

    Facilitate circles, mentor newcomers, volunteer, and help carry the tradition forward.
