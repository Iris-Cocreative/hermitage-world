Eight Around One

Six studies of the same figure — eight circles gathered around a larger one. The hermit at the center; the community, the eight directions, the eight watches of the day arranged around the stillness. Each study is interactive: walk through the rooms and touch the marks.

HERMITAGE WORLD

Study I

Convocation

The quietest form: line-work only, drawn at the same 1.7 stroke as the five value symbols. Eight seats rest on the rim of one open circle — nothing filled, nothing decorated, bronze on plaster like hardware set into a wall.

Move your pointer around the ring — whichever seat you stand nearest is lit amber, like walking the circle and greeting each one.

HERMITAGE WORLD

Study II

Clerestory

The mark as glass. Nine round panes punched through a dark wall — the large pane amber, the ring carrying the three glass colors of the aperture system. Unlit, the mark is nearly invisible; lit, it becomes the site's one authored moment in miniature.

Click the mark to ignite it — the center blooms first, then the ring lights around it, staggered like the apertures entering a dark room. Click again to let it go dark.

HERMITAGE WORLD

Study III

The Walk

Eight solid stones on a faint path, one of them amber — the keystone, the position of the founder or the present day. The ring turns almost imperceptibly on its own, a procession measured in minutes, not seconds.

Drag the ring to turn it by hand — it carries your momentum, then settles back into its slow walk.

HERMITAGE WORLD

Study IV

Breath

The inversion of Study I: the center is solid ink — mass, presence — and the eight are open circles, unclosed vessels. At rest it is a static mark. Awakened, the ring breathes: the eight drift outward on the inhale and return on the exhale, an eight-second cycle borrowed from contemplative practice.

Click to begin the breath; click again to still it.

HERMITAGE WORLD

Study V

Eclipse

The center is a full amber sun; the eight around it are the same circle in eight phases, from dark to full — the stages of a practice, or a life, arranged as a cycle. No two satellites are alike, yet all are one shape.

Click the mark to advance every phase one station around the ring.

Study VI

Punched

The mark as architecture rather than graphic: nine circular recesses carved into board-formed concrete, depth modeled with inset shadow and an opposing highlight — the Carved-Not-Cast rule made literal. This is the version that could be cast into the building itself.

Move your pointer across the wall — it becomes the sun, and the shadows inside each recess turn to follow the light.

Notes

All six studies share one geometry — a 44-unit center, eight 14-unit satellites on a 78-unit ring — so any pair can be blended. Interactions here are exploratory sketches; the shipped mark would follow the system's one-motion rule (ignition only). Under reduced-motion preferences, every study renders in its resting or fully lit state.
