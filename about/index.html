An interfaith center for meditation and spirituality

Broader than a single tradition, deeper than a wellness app. People come for the meditation and stay for the spirituality, and for each other.

“The Hermitage exists to help every person awaken to the divine energy within, so that through meditation, simplicity, and community, we may each live with greater love, peace, and purpose.”

Our mission final wording with the board

Hermitage

The quiet space within each human being. Meditation is how you find it; community is what holds you there.

World

Building a better world consciously, starting with inner transformation. Friar Justin hoped to help as many people meditate daily as possible, so that we could have world peace.

Our story

From a retreat house in Indianapolis, to seven wooded acres on Fall Creek, to a worldwide community that meets every week.

Friar Justin’s life
  1. 1979

    Alverna Retreat House

    Friar Justin begins teaching Success: Full Living and meditation in Indianapolis.

  2. 1985

    The Franciscan Hermitage

    With a few volunteers and supporters, he founds the Hermitage as an independent not-for-profit, soon at home on Fall Creek.

  3. 2015

    Thirty years, a million people

    Classes, retreats, radio and television in many countries have reached more than a million people.

  4. 2020

    The circle goes online

    The weekly Energy Circle becomes worldwide on Zoom.

  5. 2023

    Justin’s passing

    Friar Justin dies on October 13, at 88, shortly after a final retreat to Assisi.

  6. 2026

    Hermitage World

    The building is released; the Hermitage meets at the Indiana Interchurch Center and online everywhere. hermitageindy.org becomes hermitageworld.org.
