## Users

- **The Seeker (primary):** 35–65 (plus tech-raised younger seekers), spiritually curious but not religious in the traditional sense. Often ex-Catholic/Protestant/Mormon or from no tradition; carrying deep questions their old container doesn't hold; wounded by or done with organized religion but hungry for depth. Feels *alone* in their spiritual longing. Wants something deeper than a wellness app; wants companions, not doctrine.
- **The existing international community:** decades-long students of Friar Justin in Indianapolis, Australia, Europe, US. Meet weekly (Tuesday worldwide energy circle on Zoom since 2020). Need a digital home now that the physical building is gone (Sept 2026 move to Indiana Interchurch Center).
- **People seeking Friar Justin's recordings/teachings**, program participants, potential donors, board members, guest teachers.

## Product Purpose

HermitageWorld.org is the new digital front door for The Hermitage (Indianapolis, founded 1985 by Friar Justin Belitz, OFM — deceased). With the building gone, the website *is* the hermitage: a living center for meditation and spirituality, the carrier of the organization's transition from charismatic founder to mission-driven community. Success = a first-time visitor feels, within 20 minutes: (1) "I am not alone" in their spiritual longing; (2) cherished belonging — belonging through shared human seeking, not doctrine; (3) a gentle path — stories, practices, gatherings, companions. Emotional arc: *from spiritual isolation → to hopeful participation in a contemplative community.*

## Positioning

An interfaith center for meditation and spirituality, uniquely balanced between breadth and depth: broader than a Buddhist center or Catholic retreat house, more centered than Shift Network/Sounds True/Insight Timer. Core mechanism: "the hermitage is a quiet space within each human being" (Friar Justin) — meditation is how you find it; community is what holds you there. People come for the meditation and stay for the spirituality. Mission essence (agreed): *"The Hermitage exists to help every person awaken to the divine energy within — so that through meditation, simplicity, and community, we may each live with greater love, peace, and purpose."* North-star takeaway for a first visit: **"I am not alone."**

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Hermitage World**. Hermitage = the quiet space within each person; World = building a better world consciously, starting with inner transformation.
- Voice: **Warm. Grounded. Compelling.** Someone fully present with you, quiet confidence, emotional intelligence, just enough spark to keep you leaning in. NOT: cold/academic, preachy/authoritative, hype/"guru energy", passive/vague. Avoid new-age clichés, heavy spiritual language, "churchy" tone — yet carry genuine spiritual weight.
- Five core values (homepage anchors, each pairable with a symbol): **Inner divinity & unlimited potential · Personal experience over doctrine · Radical acceptance · Self-responsibility & conscious creation · Unity & connection.**
- Spatial metaphor from vision sessions: the site as *architecture rather than graphic design* — a facade, a door, a vestibule that welcomes everyone, then clear hallways to each visitor's path. Garry's image: the Indianapolis Airport atrium — vast glass-and-beam space, infinite ceiling, awe without religiosity. Samuel: classical/world sacred architecture (Renaissance, Timurid, Ottoman, Mughal, Ming — shared pursuit of symmetry, proportion, cosmic harmony) *crossed with Franciscan nature* — the architecture of a forest; geometry meeting organic form. Inspiring, clean, inviting; sacred without announcing itself; explicitly NOT new-age.
- Friar Justin is honored as founder and living lineage — a hall of remembrance / living repository — but the site is NOT a memorial; mission leads, founder grounds.
- Interfaith, non-denominational: all traditions honored ("truth is one; God is one; human expressions are ways of becoming acquainted with the One").

## Product Principles

1. **"I am not alone" before anything else** — every surface's first job is recognition and welcome, not information or conversion.
2. **Architecture, not brochure** — the site is a space entered through a threshold, with clear hallways per visitor type (newcomer / practitioner / Justin's community / donor).
3. **Come for meditation, stay for spirituality** — practice is the front door; depth reveals progressively, never dumped upfront.
4. **Founder grounds, mission leads** — Justin's presence is felt as lineage and library, never as memorial or shrine.
5. **Belonging without doctrine** — interfaith breadth expressed through shared human seeking; nothing that reads as recruitment or creed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience skews 35–65+: generous type sizes, high contrast, calm motion (respect prefers-reduced-motion), no autoplaying media. Interfaith inclusivity extends to imagery — no single tradition's iconography dominating.
