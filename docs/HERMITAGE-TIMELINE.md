## Working rhythm

- One weekly working session, 60 minutes, Tuesdays 11:00 ET, Sept 15 through Oct 27. Seven sessions total.
- Everything else asynchronous in Collab OS: comments on cards, notes on the canvas, a meeting artifact after each call.
- Working group is Garry, Samuel, James. The board hears about it at launch.
- Iris team: James throughout; Munia on page design Sept 22 – Oct 9; Sean on build Oct 12 – 27.

## Risks and how the plan absorbs them

- **Copy lands late.** Build starts Oct 12 regardless, on the pages that have copy; the rest loads in week two. Anything still missing on Oct 20 ships with the honest pending frame and drops in after launch.
- **Media cleanup runs long.** The library launches with the first clean set; the catalog grows through the CMS.
- **A design wave slips.** Wave three pages (Community, About, Give) are the simplest; they can be built from the system without a full mockup if needed.
- **Two-week slip anywhere** moves launch to Nov 10, still before the holidays, rather than shortening QA.
