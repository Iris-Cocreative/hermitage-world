The loggia

A live scene, drawn in the browser: a limestone loggia high above a sea of islands at sunset. Scroll to walk from the room, through the portal and down the vaulted gallery to the far arch. On a computer, the pointer turns your head a little.

The view beyond the arches is a photograph; everything else (the stone, the olive trees, the sunlight in the air) is generated as the page runs. The sun's light and shadows follow the sun in the photograph.
