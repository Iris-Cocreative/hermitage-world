    How access works

    Free is free to everyone, no account needed. Small cost is a one-time purchase: $3.99 a track, $15 an album, as on the old shop. Being prepared means the recording is being cleaned and titled. A Friend’s account is free and keeps a shelf; a Family membership would include everything. membership is a proposal

    Whose voice

    Every item names who made it. The old shop credits most volumes only to the Hermitage Meditation Series; they are shelved with Friar Justin’s own work until Garry confirms the voices. Books the community read together always name their author, and sit beside the circle that read them.

    Companions

    Many recordings were made in pairs: morning and evening, for one person and for a group, an explanation and then the experience. Each item shows its companions, the album it belongs to, the other forms of the same teaching, and the paths it lies on.
