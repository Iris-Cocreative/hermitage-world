Design System v0.1 · Concrete & Light

The hermitage as a modern chapel: vast quiet planes of warm material, and light entering through deliberate apertures. Sacred without announcing itself; interfaith by construction: mass, proportion, and light belong to no single tradition. Everything below is a material in that building.

Materials

Colors are named as materials because they behave like materials: grounds own whole sections, never stripes or accents. A page is a sequence of rooms: light, passage, shadow, light again.

Glass light

Light is the only decoration this system allows, and it is always authored: it comes through an aperture, from a doorway, or off a control. Amber is the hand of the place: every primary action is amber. Blue and madder appear only as glass, never as UI.

The aperture

The system's signature element: a splayed opening punched through the wall, with glass set deep in the reveal. On light grounds it reads as shadow; in dark rooms it is lit. The passage between those two states is the site's one authored motion: apertures ignite as the visitor enters a dark section, in staggered order.

Rules: apertures are square, never rounded. Scatter compositions are hand-placed and asymmetric, like Ronchamp's south wall, never grids. A lit aperture may hold a symbol; an unlit one is always empty.

Typography

Marcellus: display & headings. Lapidary Roman capitals: letterforms that could be cut into concrete. One weight; scale and case do all the work.

Awaken the divine energy within

Marcellus, inscription style: uppercase, tracked .22em. A standalone wayfinding element (dedications, labels carved beside doorways). Never placed as a small label above a heading.

The door is open to everyone

Alegreya italic: the founder's voice. Reserved exclusively for quotation: Friar Justin, the mission, endorsers. If it isn't a person speaking, it isn't italic serif.

“The hermitage is a quiet space within each human being.”

Alegreya Sans: body & interface. Humanist warmth at reading sizes; 400 / 500 / 700.

Hermitage World is a worldwide community of seekers learning to find the quiet space within, through meditation, simplicity, and companions on the way. Whatever tradition you come from, or none at all: the door is open.

Motion

  • One authored moment: the passage from daylight into the lit interior. Apertures ignite (staggered, 220ms apart) when their room enters view. Nothing else glows, pulses, or floats.
  • Reveals rise 14px on an exponential ease-out (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), 900ms). Content is visible without JavaScript.
  • Reduced motion: every aperture is simply lit; no transitions, no smooth scroll.
  • Hover is architectural: portals lift 4px and warm from below, like a doorway catching light; never scale, never bounce.

Voice

Warm · Grounded · Compelling

  • Speak like someone fully present with one person: “you,” never “users” or “visitors.”
  • Quiet confidence: short declaratives, no over-explaining. “The door has no lock, and never has.”
  • Genuine spiritual weight carried by plain words: longing, silence, belonging, peace.
  • Friar Justin speaks only in his own recorded words, always in the voice style (Alegreya italic) and cited.

Never

  • New-age cliché or “guru energy”: no journeys of transformation, no unlocking your potential.
  • Churchy or doctrinal tone; nothing that reads as recruitment or creed.
  • Hype, exclamation points, forced inspiration.
  • Cold institutional distance: this is a home, not an organization page.

The one sentence every page must be able to whisper: you are not alone.
