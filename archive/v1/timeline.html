Seven weeks to the front door

Seven Tuesday sessions, one launch. Brand and homepage settle in the first two weeks; every launch page is designed before build starts; content and media start now and run six weeks; Webflow takes two; the site goes live on the seventh Tuesday.

LaunchTuesday, October 27, 2026

This is a solid site the Hermitage can start using, not everything the sitemap holds. What isn't in the launch scope follows through the CMS and short rounds after launch. Dates move when the working group says so.

Working rhythm

  • Seven Tuesday sessions, 60 minutes, 11:00 ET, Sept 15 through Oct 27.
  • Everything else in Collab OS. Comments on cards, notes on the canvas, a meeting artifact after each call.
  • Working group: Garry, Samuel, James. The board hears about it at launch.
  • Iris team: James throughout; Munia on page design Sept 22 – Oct 9; Sean on build Oct 12 – 27.

Risks, and how the plan absorbs them

  • Copy lands late. Build starts Oct 12 on the pages that have copy; the rest loads in week two. Anything still missing on Oct 20 ships with the honest pending frame.
  • Media cleanup runs long. The library launches with the first clean set; the catalog grows through the CMS.
  • A design wave slips. The third-wave pages are the simplest and can be built from the system without a full mockup.
  • Two-week slip anywhere moves launch to Nov 10, still well before the holidays, rather than shortening QA.
One way or another, we are moving front doors online.
Samuel Robinson, August 4 review
