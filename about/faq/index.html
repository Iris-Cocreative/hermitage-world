About the Hermitage

It is spiritual and interfaith. It grew from the Franciscan tradition of its founder, Friar Justin Belitz, and it honors every tradition and none. Nothing here asks for belief; everything invites practice.

Online, every week, from anywhere; and in person in Indianapolis at the Indiana Interchurch Center, 1100 West 42nd Street. Visiting

In September 2026 the Hermitage left its longtime home and moved to the Interchurch Center. It is not an ending. Justin always said the hermitage is a quiet space within each person, and that goes wherever we do.
