The Hermitage was never the building.

For forty years, a house in Indianapolis held this community. In September it passes to new hands. What Friar Justin actually built keeps going: people in the United States, Australia, and Europe who found the quiet space within, and found each other. Come for the meditation. Stay for the spirituality.

One letter a week. Quiet, useful, easy to leave.

Founded on a friar's conviction that peace is learnable.

Justin Belitz was a Franciscan friar who taught meditation across the United States, Europe, and Australia for four decades, and believed every person could awaken to the divine energy within. This is not a memorial to him. It is his work, continuing.
