## 2. Who we are

> The Hermitage exists to help every person awaken to the divine energy within, so that through meditation, simplicity, and community, we may each live with greater love, peace, and purpose.

Mission essence, agreed with Garry and Samuel (board wording still open).

**North star.** "I am not alone." The one thing a first visit must leave behind. *Hermitage* = the quiet space within each person. *World* = building a better world consciously, starting with inner transformation.

**Five values, five lights.** Inner divinity & unlimited potential · Personal experience over doctrine · Radical acceptance · Self-responsibility & conscious creation · Unity & connection. Each value has a symbol that lives inside a lit aperture (see §7).

## 3. Voice

**Warm. Grounded. Compelling.** Someone fully present with you. Quiet confidence, emotional intelligence, and just enough spark to keep you leaning in. Genuine spiritual weight without heavy spiritual language.

Sounds like:
- Plain words carrying real weight: "You are not alone in your longing."
- Invitation before information: the door has no lock, and never has.
- Practice first, depth revealed slowly: come for meditation, stay for spirituality.
- Interfaith by construction: truth is one; human expressions are ways of becoming acquainted with the One.
- The founder as living lineage: what can these thoughts do for you?

Never sounds like:
- Cold or academic.
- Preachy or authoritative; nothing that reads as recruitment or creed.
- Hype or "guru energy."
- Passive or vague.
- New-age clichés, churchy tone, pastoral tone-setting by default.
- A memorial. Justin is foundation, not shrine (Garry, 8/4).

Anchor quote, and the only kind of text set in Alegreya italic: *"The hermitage is a quiet space within each human being."* Friar Justin Belitz, OFM.

## 4. The mark

**Eight around one, refreshed.** Two leading candidates from the September studies. Both keep the nine circles Garry asked us not to throw out.

- **Candidate A · Study IX, The Ninth Seat.** The friar figure (cleared by the Franciscans) takes one seat on the ring; the center is left open, the quiet space within each person that no one occupies. Justin as one of us, walking the circle.
- **Candidate B · Study VII, The Walk, refined.** The same mark with the seat filled, for contexts where the figure is too much. Garry's August favorite tightened into a proportion system.

**Proportion system.** One module *m* = seat radius (14). Core radius = π·m (44). Ring radius = 5.57·m (78). Stroke = m ÷ 8 (1.7). Eight seats at 45°, first seat at the top.

**Clear space.** One seat diameter (2m) on every side.

**Minimum sizes.** 64 · 40 · 24 · 16. Below 40px the friar simplifies to a solid seat and the core stroke thickens (the "small" cut). Never scale the full mark below 40px.

**Misuse.**
- Do not rotate the ring; the open or friar seat sits at the top.
- Do not add gradients, bevels or shadows to the mark; light lives in the aperture, not the logo.
- Do not set the wordmark in any face but Marcellus, and never rounded or bold.
- Do not recolor outside Materials and Glass: bronze or ink on plaster, lumen or amber on shadow.
- Do not place the mark on busy photography; give it a plaster or shadow ground.
- Do not pair it with the old teal, or with the "The Hermitage" two-weight wordmark.
- Do not draw the friar any other way; the figure is the stained-glass one, simplified, never a cartoon.

**Decision still open:** which candidate, and whether the figure belongs in the primary mark or only on Friar Justin surfaces.

**Lockups (Figma, section 04).** Horizontal (mark 160 beside HERMITAGE WORLD, Marcellus 34, tracked 18%), stacked on shadow (mark 170 over two lines, Marcellus 26, tracked 20%), nav (simplified mark at 24px beside the inscription-style wordmark), favicon 32 dark and light, app icon 64.

## 5. Color

Every value is warm-biased; even the darks are umber, never blue-black. Colors behave like building materials: a ground owns a whole section.

**Four rules.**
- *The Grounds Rule.* A color is a material that owns a full-bleed section; never a stripe, chip or accent inside another ground.
- *The Glass-Only Rule.* Blue and madder never touch the UI. If it is clickable, focusable or a state, it is amber (form errors excepted).
- *The Warm Dark Rule.* No neutral grays and no blue-blacks. Secondary text is ink-soft or lumen-soft; darks are umber.
- *The Modeling Ramp Rule.* Pure black and white never appear as surfaces or text; only as translucent steps that carve depth and light.

## 6. Typography

**Lapidary capitals over humanist warmth.**

- **Display** · Marcellus 400 · clamp(2.6rem, 6.4vw, 5.5rem) · hero headline only.
- **Headline** · Marcellus 400 · clamp(1.9rem, 3.6vw, 2.9rem).
- **Body** · Alegreya Sans 400 · 1.125rem / 1.65 · measure 66ch.
- **Voice** · Alegreya italic 400 · only when a person is speaking, always cited.
- **Inscription** · Marcellus 400 · 0.8125rem · uppercase · tracked 0.22em · a standalone architectural element, never an eyebrow.

Families: Marcellus (display, headline, inscription, wordmark) · Alegreya Sans 400 / 500 / 700 (body, interface) · Alegreya Italic (voice). Fallbacks: Times New Roman · Segoe UI · Georgia.

**Three rules.**
- *The Voice Rule.* Alegreya italic appears exclusively as quotation: Friar Justin, the mission, named endorsers. If it is not a person speaking, it is not italic serif.
- *The Standalone Inscription Rule.* The inscription style is wayfinding, day markers, form labels, dedications. It is never a small label above a heading. This system has no eyebrows or kickers.
- *The One-Weight Rule.* Marcellus ships in one weight. Emphasis within display type comes from size, case and tracking, never boldness.

## 7. The aperture

**Light is the only decoration, and it is always authored.**

**Anatomy.** Three layers: the outer reveal (135° gradient, deep inset shadow), an intermediate splay (inset 11%, opposing 315°), and the glass pane (inset 26%, near-black radial). The wall reads thick; unlit, the opening reads as shadow.

**Lit.** The pane blooms from a near-white hotspot (#FFF8E6) through the glass color, gains an inner glow and a halo cast into the room. Transition 0.9s, ease-out. Variants: amber (default), glass blue, madder. Blue and madder exist only as glass.

**Ignition, the one authored moment.** As the visitor enters a dark room, its apertures ignite in staggered order: 200ms, then 220ms per aperture. Light grounds keep apertures unlit; the hero scatter never ignites. Under reduced-motion every aperture is simply lit on load. Nothing else on the site pulses, floats, scales or bounces.

**The five symbols.** Inner divinity (radiant point) · Experience over doctrine (own path) · Radical acceptance (all held) · Conscious creation (drawn square) · Unity & connection (shared center). One glyph per value, one 1.7 stroke, geometric and tradition-neutral. They live inside lit apertures and may appear alone as bronze line-work at small sizes. No other icon system exists.

## 8. Imagery: the architectural palette

**A virtual space, not a brochure.** Agreed with Garry and Samuel on 8/4: develop an architectural palette so that generated and photographed imagery genuinely feels like the Hermitage as a place, some rooms bright and full of light, others dark and contemplative. Forward-looking, not pastoral (Lulu to weigh in on nature imagery).

- **Squares with light streaming.** Garry: "there are your squares." The textured reading of the hero: board-formed concrete, punched openings, a shaft of sun.
- **Vast openings, figures looking up.** Samuel: the spiritual seeker. "I'm here, there's just all this here, and I'm engaging with it." Atrium volume, infinite ceiling, awe without religiosity.
- **Pattern.** The patterned ceiling Garry responded to: world sacred geometry (Timurid, Ottoman, Mughal, Ming) crossed with Franciscan nature. Parametric patterns from James's tool, handed over with the controls.
- **Cloth and light.** A creased cloth with light running through it. Texture that reads as material, never as filter or gradient.
- **Real people, real place.** Photographs from in-person gatherings prove there are real people meeting in a real place, even to someone on the other side of the world. The in-person side feeds the online front door.
- **The non-linear path.** Monument Valley, the elephant path, the confessional seat that reads as "take a seat and talk to Justin." Sideways and upside-down parts; the community walks with you every step.

Never: stock imagery · sage gradients · lotus minimalism · AI-generated likenesses of Friar Justin · pastoral tone-setting by default · imagery where one tradition's iconography dominates.

## 9. Do and don't

**Do**
- Give every section a single material ground and switch text palettes on shadow; sequence light and dark sections like rooms.
- Make every interactive accent amber: links amber-deep on plaster, amber-bright on shadow; focus rings 2px amber.
- Hand-place aperture scatters asymmetrically, like Ronchamp's south wall; reserve ignition for dark sections.
- Keep all content visible without JavaScript; motion only adds choreography.
- Hold missing photography with the labeled pending-asset frame, naming the asset and its source.
- Use inscriptions as standalone architectural elements: wayfinding, day markers, form labels, dedications.
- Carve depth inward with inset shadows; let outward shadow exist only as light or as state.

**Don't**
- Round a corner. No border-radius on any surface (the 1px focus-ring softening is the sole exception).
- Put glass blue or madder on anything interactive or textual; glass only.
- Place an eyebrow, kicker, or small label above a heading.
- Add motion beyond the one authored moment: nothing pulses, floats, scales or bounces.
- Use gray. Secondary text is warm; darks are umber.
- Box schedules or lists into cards; use hairline-separated boards.
- Use stock imagery, AI-generated likenesses of Friar Justin, or a bold Marcellus.
- Fake a working backend; prototype forms and disabled previews say what they are.

## Typography proposals

Figma page *Typography Proposals*: four full specimen frames (display, headline, inscription, lede, body, quoted voice with cite, button, nav row), each with rationale and a fit note for a 35–65+ audience, then a comparison frame and recommendation. All fonts loaded from Google Fonts; none failed.

- **Pairing A · Current.** Marcellus + Alegreya Sans + Alegreya italic. Lapidary capitals over a humanist sans; one weight of Marcellus so hierarchy is size, case, tracking. Fit: strong; Alegreya Sans has a large x-height and open apertures at 18px; Marcellus never below 13px.
- **Pairing B · Geometric quiet.** Jost Light (display, tracked caps) + Source Serif 4 (body) + Source Serif 4 italic (voice). Circles and squares in the letters rhyme with the round-two marks. Fit: good with care; Jost Light never below 32px or on shadow below 40px; use Regular for inscriptions and UI.
- **Pairing C · Stone and hand.** Cormorant Garamond (display, large) + Atkinson Hyperlegible (body) + Cormorant italic (voice). Fit: the most legible body text of the four; the most fragile display face (at least 40px, sentence case, untracked, never on shadow below 48px).
- **Pairing D · Humanist classical.** Cardo (display and voice italic) + Figtree (body/UI). A scholar's Bembo over a friendly sans. Fit: good; keep Figtree Regular/Medium; watch Cardo's tight line-height at display sizes.

**Recommendation.** Keep A as the working system; take B into the next design round as the one real alternative, mocked up beside A on a full page if the geometric marks win. C belongs in the accessibility conversation, not the brand one. D is handsome but generic.

## Iconography

Figma page *Iconography*: a 24px-grid icon system built from the same circle and square as the mark, one stroke 1.5 at 24, drawn as 31 Figma components (`Icon/…`) shown in a labeled grid.

- **Anchor row (the five value symbols):** Inner divinity · Experience over doctrine · Radical acceptance · Conscious creation · Unity & connection.
- **UI icons (26):** Meditation, Programs, Gatherings, Calendar, In person, Friar Justin / Library, Community, Give, Newsletter, Search, Menu, Close, Arrow right, External link, Play, Pause, Audio, Video, Duration, Free tag, Download, Online / Zoom, Location, Time zone, Retreat, Book.
- **How icons are built:** grid 24; keylines 20 square (2px inset) and 20 circle; live area 18–20; one stroke 1.5 (2 at 32, 1 at 16); square corners, square terminals, miter joins; only circles and straight lines; filled dots (r about 1.3) are the only solid shapes; optical centering; color inherited from adjacent text, amber only when the icon is the action; the five value symbols are the anchors every new icon must sit beside.
- **In lit apertures:** icons read dark umber (#241A08) on amber glass, as the value symbols do.
- **Pattern:** two tileable motifs for backgrounds, 120px repeat. Tile A, nine-square lattice (cells at 12% ink, center cell as a hairline). Tile B, ring lattice (eight seats on a 42 ring, hairline bronze at 60%).
