The Hermitage was never the building.

For forty years, a house in Indianapolis held this community. In September it passes to new hands. What Friar Justin actually built keeps going: people in the United States, Australia, and Europe who found the quiet space within, and found each other. Eight seats around one, and one of them is open.

One letter a week. Quiet, useful, easy to leave.

Peace is learnable. That was the founder's whole claim.

Justin Belitz was a Franciscan friar who taught meditation across the United States, Europe, and Australia for four decades. He believed every person could awaken to the divine energy within, and that enough people meditating daily would change the world. This is not a memorial to him. It is a foundation, still being built on.

The question he leaves is a practical one: what can these thoughts do for you?
