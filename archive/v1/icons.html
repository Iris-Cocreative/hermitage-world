The Five Values, Drawn

Three candidates for each value, all in one grammar: a 48-unit grid, a single 1.7 stroke, geometric primitives, and exactly one filled point in every glyph. The point is the person, the spark, the divine energy within. Read down the recommended column and the five icons tell one story: the spark shines, walks its own way, is held among all forms, builds, and joins the others. Nothing borrowed from any single tradition.

Inner divinity & unlimited potential

You already carry what you are looking for. The spark is present, radiant, and larger than any container.

Recommended

The Radiant Point

The spark at center, rays reaching for the ring: light already shining from within. The simplest true statement of the value.

The Open Vault

The vault above the spark is broken open at its crown, and light passes through: potential without a ceiling. More narrative, slightly less calm.

The Deep Well

Rings of depth around the spark, the outermost left open: the further in you go, the further it opens. Quietest of the three; reads less at small sizes.

Personal experience over doctrine

Nothing here asks for belief. The spark leaves the prescribed line and walks a path only it can walk.

Recommended

The Own Path

A dashed rule stands for the handed-down line; the spark departs it on a lived curve. The clearest possible picture of the value, and the most human glyph in the set.

The Unclosed Circle

The circle refuses to close; the spark stands in the opening. Elegant and calm, but the meaning leans abstract without a caption.

Through the Aperture

The spark enters through an opening in the wall: direct experience as the door. Ties tightly to the site's aperture grammar, at the cost of repeating it.

Radical acceptance

There is beauty in everyone and everything. Every form belongs inside the circle, unchanged.

Recommended

All Held

One ring holds three unlike forms: the point, the square, the triangle, none asked to become the others. Acceptance of difference drawn literally, and instantly legible.

The Vesica

Two equal circles sharing common ground. Beautiful and ancient, but it reads as partnership more than acceptance, and the sacred-geometry echo may pull toward the new-age register the brand avoids.

The Embrace

Two arcs curve around the spark without closing on it: held, never confined. Tender, but the open arcs can read as brackets at interface sizes.

Self-responsibility & conscious creation

Thought, intention and action shape a life. The spark is the pen, and the form is still being drawn.

Recommended

The Drawn Square

A square, deliberately unfinished, with the spark at the pen's position: your life as a form you are consciously completing. Echoes the site's aperture without copying it.

Point, Line, Plane

Creation as ascent: the point becomes a line becomes a form. A quiet Bauhaus-order statement; reads as three separate marks at small sizes.

The Builder's Spiral

A rectangular spiral growing outward from the spark: creation compounding, turn by turn. Strong at large sizes; densest of the three when small.

Unity & connection

All life is held together by love. The sparks are many, and they are joined.

Recommended

The Shared Center

Five sparks joined through one center: unity as a common heart, not a closed club. Calm, symmetric, and the filled center quietly rhymes with Inner Divinity, closing the set's story where it began.

The Ring of Five

Five sparks joined hand to hand in a ring: community as a circle of equals. Honest and warm; slightly generic next to the shared-center form.

One Thread

A single line passing through five sparks: connection as one continuous thread of life. Lyrical and distinctive; the least symmetric, and it wants horizontal room.

The recommended set, together

Read left to right, the five glyphs tell the seeker's story in the brand's own grammar: the spark shines within, walks its own path, is held among all forms, builds a life, and joins the others through a shared heart. One stroke, one grid, one filled point each. Small sizes use a 2.4 stroke cut of the same drawings.

Two of the five (The Radiant Point, The Drawn Square) are refinements of the glyphs already live on the homepage; choosing this set would swap in The Own Path, All Held, and The Shared Center. Any candidate above can be substituted; the grammar holds regardless of the mix.
