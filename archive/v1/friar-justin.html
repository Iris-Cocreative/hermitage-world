Friar Justin Belitz, OFM

Franciscan friar. Teacher of meditation on three continents. Founder of the Hermitage, 1985. He spent his life convinced of one simple thing:

“The purpose of the Hermitage is to help as many people meditate daily so we can have world peace.” Friar Justin Belitz, OFM

He left a path, not a doctrine.

Justin rarely explained it outright, but everything he taught follows what he called the prayer outline: a movement every person can walk at their own pace, from words, toward images, into silence.

First

Spoken Prayer

Where most of us begin: the prayers of childhood, words said aloud. Nothing to unlearn. A doorway, honored as one.

Then

Mental Prayer

Meditation with the mind's eye: visualization, affirmation, imagination in service of a full life. The heart of Success: Full Living.

Finally

The Prayer of Silence

Beyond words and images: quiet contemplation, resting in the hermitage within. The capstone he called Living the Prayer of Quiet.

Twenty-five hours of his voice, kept living.

Recorded simply, an iPad on a stand, a friar who kept teaching through everything. Being restored now, homily by homily. AI helps us search it; it never speaks for him.

This hall is not a memorial. It is a doorway he built for you.

Justin never wanted followers looking back at him. He wanted meditators: people who find the quiet space within and carry peace out into their families, their work, their world. The best way to remember him is to begin.
